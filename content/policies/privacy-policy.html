Who we share it with

We share data only with processors who need it to run the shop: our e-commerce platform (Shopify), payment providers, delivery carriers (An Post, DPD), our email marketing provider, and analytics providers where you have consented. We do not sell your personal data.

Transfers outside the EEA

Some of our processors are based outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens we rely on an adequacy decision from the European Commission, or on Standard Contractual Clauses together with any additional safeguards needed. You can ask us for details of the safeguards in place.

How long we keep it

  • Order and transaction records — six years, to meet Irish tax and accounting requirements.
  • Account data — while your account is open, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Marketing data — until you unsubscribe, then suppressed so we do not email you again.
  • Support correspondence — two years from the last message.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to: access the data we hold about you; have inaccurate data corrected; have data erased; restrict or object to processing; receive your data in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time (without affecting processing already carried out). To exercise any of these, email [DPO EMAIL]. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28 — dataprotection.ie.

Children

This store is not intended for children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
