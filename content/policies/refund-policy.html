Your right to cancel (14 days)

As a consumer buying at a distance, you have the right under EU law and the Irish Consumer Rights Act 2022 to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason. The period ends 14 days after the day you, or someone you nominate, physically receives the goods. Where an order arrives in several parcels, it runs from the day you receive the last item.

To cancel, make a clear statement to that effect before the 14 days are up: email info.dressar@gmail.com with your order number. You may use the model cancellation form below, but you do not have to.

Our returns policy (30 days)

We voluntarily extend that period. You have 30 days from delivery to return anything you have changed your mind about, provided it is unworn, unwashed, has its tags attached and is in a resaleable condition. This is in addition to, and does not limit, your statutory rights.

Sending it back

Once you have told us you are cancelling or returning, you have 14 days to send the goods back to [RETURNS ADDRESS]. Use a tracked service and keep your proof of postage — until we receive the parcel, it is your responsibility. Return postage is at your own cost, unless the item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered.

Refunds

We refund to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving the goods back, or of you supplying evidence that you have sent them, whichever comes first. The refund includes the standard outbound delivery charge you paid. If you chose a more expensive delivery option, we refund the standard rate only. We may reduce a refund to reflect handling beyond what is needed to establish the nature and characteristics of the item.

Exceptions

The right to cancel does not apply to sealed underwear or swimwear where the hygiene seal has been broken, to pierced jewellery, or to made-to-order and altered pieces. Nothing here affects your rights if goods are faulty, not as described, or unfit for purpose.

Faulty or incorrect items

If an item is faulty, damaged in transit, or is not what you ordered, contact us and we will arrange a repair, replacement or refund at our cost, including return postage. Your remedies under the Consumer Rights Act 2022 extend well beyond 30 days.

Exchanges

The quickest way to exchange is to return the item for a refund and place a new order, so the size you want is not sold out while your parcel is in transit.
