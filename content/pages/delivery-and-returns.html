No customs charges

We hold stock inside the European Union and ship to you from within the single market. Your order will not attract customs duty or an import handling fee, and there is nothing to pay on delivery.

VAT

All prices shown include Irish VAT at the standard rate of 23%, which applies to adult clothing. Children's clothing and footwear for children under 11 is zero-rated in Ireland, and where an item qualifies the 0% rate is already reflected in the price you see. A full VAT breakdown appears on your order confirmation.

Returns and your right to cancel

Your legal right (14 days)

Because you are buying at a distance, EU consumer law and the Irish Consumer Rights Act 2022 give you the right to cancel this contract within 14 days without giving any reason. That period runs from the day you (or someone you nominate) physically receive the goods. To exercise it, tell us clearly before the 14 days are up — email hello@dressar.eu or use the cancellation form linked below. You then have a further 14 days to send the goods back.

Our policy (30 days)

We go further than the law requires: you have 30 days from delivery to return anything you have changed your mind about. Items must be unworn and unwashed, with tags still attached and in a condition we can resell.

What it costs

Return postage is paid by you unless the item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered — in which case we cover it. We refund to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving the goods back, or of you proving you have sent them, whichever is sooner. The refund includes standard outbound delivery; if you chose an upgraded service we refund the standard rate.

What cannot be returned

For hygiene reasons we cannot accept returns of pierced jewellery, or of underwear and swimwear where the hygiene seal has been broken. Made-to-order and altered pieces are also excluded. None of this affects your statutory rights if an item is faulty.

Faulty or incorrect items

If something arrives damaged, faulty or is not what you ordered, contact us within a reasonable time and we will put it right — repair, replacement, or refund. Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2022 last well beyond 30 days and are not limited by our returns window.

How to return

  1. Email hello@dressar.eu with your order number and what you are sending back.
  2. We reply with a return address and instructions within one working day.
  3. Post the parcel using a tracked service and keep your proof of postage.
  4. We confirm by email when it arrives and process your refund.

Complaints

If something goes wrong and we cannot resolve it between us, you can contact the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC) at ccpc.ie, or use the European Commission's online dispute resolution platform.
