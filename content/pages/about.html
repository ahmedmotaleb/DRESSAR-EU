Dressar makes modest clothing for women who want coverage without compromise — pieces that are quiet, well cut and actually wearable on an ordinary Tuesday.

Where we started

We began in Cairo, designing and producing in small runs so that fabric, drape and length could get proper attention. That has not changed: every piece still starts as a sketch in our studio, and we still make in quantities small enough that we can fix a hemline between production runs.

Why Ireland

Irish customers were finding us long before we had a way to serve them properly — ordering from abroad, waiting weeks, and sometimes getting caught by customs charges at the door. So we set up here: EU stock, Irish VAT included in the price, delivery in a few days, and returns to an address that does not involve an international courier.

How we cut

Modest does not have to mean shapeless. We work to a few rules: opaque fabrics or a full lining, sleeves that stay where you put them, considered lengths, and a fit that skims rather than clings. Where a piece is sheer, it is lined. Where a skirt is long, it is long enough.

What we are working on

Better size range, more natural fibres, and packaging we are not embarrassed by. If there is something you want us to make — or a fit that has never quite worked for you — tell us at hello@dressar.ie. We read all of it.
