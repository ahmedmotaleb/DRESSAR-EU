How to measure

  • Bust — around the fullest part, tape level and not pulled tight.
  • Waist — the narrowest part of your torso, usually just above the navel.
  • Hip — around the fullest part, roughly 20 cm below the waist.
  • Height — useful for maxi lengths. Our maxi styles are drafted for 165–172 cm.

Fabric and fit notes

  • Linen and cotton relax slightly with wear and may shrink a little on a warm wash. Wash cool.
  • Chiffon and satin are cut generously and fully lined where the fabric is sheer.
  • Crochet and knit cardigans are one-size-generous and drape over most sizes.
